About

I lead EHCP document validation at a national SEND education provider: transformer models over clinical and educational text, whose outputs inform annual funding decisions. High-stakes settings are my favourite place to do ML precisely because "it seems to work" isn't good enough there.

I hold an MSc in Data Science & AI (Distinction) and I'm a founding board member at SRHIN, a health NGO working on AI-driven health equity in West Africa. Before data science I trained as a chemical engineer, so I've always thought in pipelines. My Medium bio has said the same thing for years: intrigued by the human experience, musing on practical applications of data science.

Away from the terminal I'm usually at the other kind of keyboard: I play and write music.
